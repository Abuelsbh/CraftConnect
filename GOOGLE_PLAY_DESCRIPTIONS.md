## Full Description (4000 characters max)

**PIX & FIX - Your Trusted Connection to Local Artisans**

Find skilled professionals in your area for all your repair, maintenance, and service needs. PIX & FIX connects you with verified local artisans and craftsmen, making it easy to get quality services whenever you need them.

**🎯 Key Features:**

**Find Local Professionals**
- Browse a wide network of skilled artisans in your area
- Search by craft type: Electricians, Plumbers, Carpenters, Painters, Mechanics, HVAC Technicians, Satellite Installers, Internet Technicians, Tilers, Locksmiths, and more
- View detailed artisan profiles with experience, ratings, and portfolios

**Interactive Maps**
- Discover nearby artisans on interactive Google Maps
- Get directions to artisan locations
- Find professionals sorted by distance from your location
- See real-time availability and service areas

**Instant Communication**
- Chat directly with artisans in real-time
- Send messages, photos, and voice recordings
- Get instant quotes for your projects
- Schedule appointments and discuss service details

**Fault Reporting System**
- Report technical issues quickly and easily
- Upload photos and voice descriptions of problems
- Schedule immediate or future service requests
- Track your service requests from start to completion
- Monitor status updates in real-time

**User-Friendly Interface**
- Modern, intuitive design that's easy to navigate
- Bilingual support: English and Arabic
- Responsive layout that works on all screen sizes
- Fast and reliable performance

**Secure & Reliable**
- Secure authentication system
- Verified artisan profiles
- Protected user data
- Safe communication channels

**Perfect For:**
- Home repairs and maintenance
- Electrical issues and installations
- Plumbing problems and installations
- Painting and renovation projects
- Car and vehicle repairs
- HVAC system maintenance
- Internet and satellite installations
- Tile and flooring work
- Lock and security services
- And much more!

Whether you need a quick fix or a major project, PIX & FIX helps you find the right professional at the right time. Download now and connect with skilled artisans in your community!
